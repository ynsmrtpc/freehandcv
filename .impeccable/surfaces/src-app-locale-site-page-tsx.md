# Surface brief: FreehandCV redesign (public site + app shell)

Scope: whole product redesign, replacing the incumbent Tailwind-SaaS look. Landing is Persuade; editor, dashboard, template editor are Operate; examples and guides are Read. Direction pinned by the user after critique (2026-11-12): "clear public-service skeleton + paper identity". No concept-seed roll (user-pinned).

Audience/job: once-a-year job seekers across the whole labour market, half on phones, mixed ages and eyesight. Job: a good PDF, free, without traps.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A job application in Turkey is a white form filled in with a blue ballpoint pen. FreehandCV is that form done right: e-Devlet-plain structure, form paper, ruled lines, ballpoint-blue ink. Refuses the category default (blue SaaS gradient hero, glow, bento, floating stat chips, icon tiles).

OWN-WORLD: cool form-paper white surfaces on a cool grey desk (never cream); graphite text; one ink colour, ballpoint violet-blue #2536a8, for actions and marks; stamp green for "approved", amber for caution. Hairline form rules (1px, blue-grey) group content instead of shadows and cards; small radii (6–10px); Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything, chosen for low-vision legibility; large labels. Signature move: the freehand ballpoint mark, a hand-drawn ink underline and tick drawn as SVG strokes, used for emphasis and for "done".

STORY: visitor sees a real CV and plain statements of what is free; believes there is no catch; starts writing in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: left, h1 with the key word underlined in ink stroke; one plain sentence; one ink primary CTA plus a quiet secondary; three ruled "no card / no watermark / no trial" lines with ink ticks. Right, the real resume paper on the desk with a template switcher as a ruled segmented row. No badge, no glow, no chips.

FORM: user-pinned (critique options: B skeleton + A paper), position 1. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
